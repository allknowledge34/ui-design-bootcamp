# Tralance — Neumorphism (Soft UI) Landing Page

A production-ready, authentic Neumorphic (Soft UI) landing page for **Tralance**, the income and expense tracker built for independent freelancers, contractors, and creators.

## Design Philosophy

- **Authentic Soft UI**: Soft dual-source shadows (light reflection top-left, darker shadow bottom-right) extruded against neutral substrate colors.
- **Streamlined Product Structure**: Two-column hero with mobile phone mockup and soft radial sunburst disc, side-by-side App Store & Google Play store buttons, ratings bar (Capterra, G2, Trustpilot), 3 tinted feature cards with circular embossed icon wells, and bills management console.
- **Concise Copy**: Clean, purposeful text without marketing fluff or bloated filler paragraphs.
- **Dark Mode**: Seamless persistent theme toggling between soft light (`#e6ecf4`) and dark slate (`#181c24`).
- **Tactile Interactions**: Realistic pressable buttons that transition to debossed inset depth on click.
